# SahaAI — AI-Powered Learning Recovery

> **Helping every child catch up without asking teachers to stop the class.**

SahaAI is an AI-powered learning recovery platform designed to help students recover learning gaps after school absence.

The project focuses on **Challenge 1C — Absenteeism Creates Learning Gaps**.

---

## 1. Problem

When a student misses school, three problems can occur:

- Teachers may not have enough time to identify exactly what the student missed.
- Individual reteaching can be difficult while managing the whole classroom.
- Continuing the class may leave the absent student behind, while repeating the lesson can disrupt the classroom pace.

SahaAI is designed to support the recovery process without requiring the teacher to stop the regular class.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

SahaAI provides an AI-assisted learning recovery workflow.

### Workflow

```text
Student Absence
       ↓
Teacher Selects Missed Lesson
       ↓
Curriculum Context Retrieval
       ↓
AI Identifies Learning Concepts
       ↓
AI Generates Recovery Plan
       ↓
Student Learn
       ↓
Student Practice
       ↓
Student Check
       ↓
Recovery Status
       ↓
Teacher Recommendation
